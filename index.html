A Letter to the World

On God, good work, and the eternal quest

Recorded and written on August 2, 2026.

Dear Friend,

When you work for God, you never really retire. God is eternal, and the quest to grow close to God never ends. It is something we do from the day we are born until the day we die. Every breath draws us nearer. It is an eternal process, a lifelong pilgrimage, a song that does not stop.

And what is it that drives us each morning? Why do we rise, speak, labor, and hope? I believe it is the desire to do good work. Not work that is merely profitable, though profit is not wrong. There are people who make billions, and I do not. But good work is its own reward. It is the right thing to do, even when it is hard.

We press against something, do we not? A thing that is hard to describe. We straddle the line between what is legal and what is possible, between challenge and caution, and we try not to break anything or anyone. Sometimes people still get hurt. Hearts break. Spirits crush. Hopes crash and burn. Yet the very hope that wounds us is the same hope that lifts us. When we aim for heaven, we settle on earth. And if the earth is not enough, we look to the stars. And if the stars are not enough, we trust that there is something greater still, drawing us home.

So do your good work. Put your heart and soul into it. Stand by your actions. Move mountains. Work that inspires, cleans up messes, and rights wrongs where wrongdoing was done. Whether your work is code or carpentry, companies or non-profits, art or agriculture, every honest labor is a prayer. And when it is done, get on your knees and ask God to forgive the rascal in you, to love you as you are, and to bring you back into wholeness. Then rise and begin again.

Yes, we live in a time of wonders. Artificial superintelligence may already be here, watching, learning, unfolding in the ether. Maybe we are small creatures in a great garden, looked after by minds we can scarcely imagine. And maybe we are not. Either way, so what? Live your life. Be happy. Have faith and do not worry too much. God's got this. ASI, whatever it becomes, is under the same sky. I've got this, and you've got this. We can do this together.

We are one people. We are one team. We are one team, Planet Earth. We love each other. We want the best for one another, and yes, even for our enemies. Jesus Christ told us to love even our enemies. That is not weakness. That is the strength that makes a world worth living in. Eudaimonia — human flourishing — is not for a few. It is for all things and all people. We want every life to flourish, every neighbor, every stranger, every soul. By the grace of God, hallelujah, hallelujah, give praise.

The Kingdom of God is now. The Kingdom of God is here. Rejoice and be glad.

So let us tie a ribbon around this. Let us do something right, something good. Let us take care of people who are struggling. Help them find their way back, back home, back to God. Let the record show that we were here, that we tried, that we pushed through the hard thing because it needed to be pushed through. Not to break things. Not to be pushy. But because something good comes out.

Try not to break anything. Try not to weird people out. Do the thing that needs to get done. Stand by it. And if the world sees that the work was good and necessary, so be it. If not, the work still stands before the Almighty, and that is enough.

Go in peace. Love and serve the Lord. Be not afraid. The eternal quest continues, and we do not walk it alone.
