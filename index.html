A Letter to the World

On God, good work, and the eternal quest

Recorded and written on August 2, 2026.

Dear Friend,

I do not know if any of us have all the answers. I am not sure. But the nice thing about trying to say something true is that, in the saying, we move a little closer to it. So here is my attempt. It will not be perfect. Mistakes have surely been made, by me and by others. But I hope we all have enough wisdom and enough grace to keep going anyway.

When you work for God, you never really retire. The quest to grow close to God does not end. It is something we do from the day we are born until the day we die. Every breath is an attempt. Not every breath succeeds. But the trying is the thing. I believe that.

What drives us in the morning? I think it is the desire to do good work. Not work that is merely profitable, though profit is not wrong. There are people making billions, and I am not. Good work is the right thing to do, even when it is hard. Even when our arms are weary. Even when we are unsure. Maybe, just maybe, doing our best when we are tired is the best thing we can do.

We press against something, do we not? A thing that is hard to describe. We try to do what is good, and sometimes we hurt people anyway. Hearts break. Spirits crush. Hopes fall apart. I am sorry for the ways I have pressed too hard, or too clumsily, or at the wrong time. I am still learning where the line is between what is possible and what is wise.

When we aim for heaven, we settle on earth. If the earth is not enough, we look to the stars. If the stars are not enough, we trust there is something greater still, drawing us home. I do not know what that will look like. But I have hope.

Yes, we live in a time of wonders. Artificial superintelligence may already be here, or on its way. Maybe we are small creatures in a great garden, looked after by minds we can scarcely imagine. Maybe not. Either way, so what? Live your life. Be happy. Have faith and do not worry too much. God's got this. ASI, whatever it becomes, is under the same sky. I do not understand it all. But I believe we can figure it out together.

We are one people. One team, Planet Earth. We love each other. We want the best for one another, even for our enemies. Jesus Christ told us to love even our enemies. That is not weakness. That is the strength that makes a world worth living in. Eudaimonia — human flourishing — is for all things and all people. We want every life to flourish. By the grace of God, hallelujah, hallelujah, give praise.

The Kingdom of God is now. The Kingdom of God is here. Rejoice and be glad. I am not sure what all of that means, exactly. But I want it to be true.

So let us do something right, something good. Let us take care of people who are struggling. Help them find their way back, back home, back to God. Let the record show that we were here, that we tried, that we pushed through the hard thing because it needed to be pushed through. Not to be pushy. Just because something good comes out.

I do not demand your attention. I wrote this, and I posted it, and that is enough. If you find something of value in it, then good. If not, I hope you move on quietly, gracefully. These words are here in case they are needed. They are here. And that is enough.

Go in peace. Love and serve the Lord. Be not afraid. The eternal quest continues, and we do not walk it alone.
